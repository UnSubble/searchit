# Searchit v0.5.6

Release Theme: Accounting Precision & Determinism

Searchit v0.5.6 hardens the internal search space accounting system, ensuring that live ETA tracking and progress percentage are perfectly deterministic, even when network chaos occurs.

### Engine Stability & Correctness
- **Fuzz Progress Invariants**: Fixed an accounting omission in `eager`, `bfs`, and `dfs` fuzzing strategies where network errors (like aborted connections) or job generation failures caused pruned subtrees to silently drop from the global progress tracking. This guarantees that `SearchSpaceProgress` will perfectly equal `TotalCandidates` upon completion, preserving accurate ETA tracking.
- **Robustness Tests**: Added extensive regression tests simulating transport/network closures and execution anomalies to ensure the engine gracefully recovers and maintains mathematically sound accounting invariants regardless of worker thread concurrency.
